Consistent hashing KV store

Routes keys to backend nodes around a hash ring. Adding or removing a node only re-maps a small fraction of keys. Click the ring to drop a key. Add/remove nodes and watch how many keys actually move.

Benchmarked with k6: 20 VUs, 60K ops, sub-500ms P95, low failure rate.
